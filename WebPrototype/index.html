Orbital Cargo Dispatch

Graveyard Shift

Crates roll in on the belt. Carry each one to the bay its colour points to, scan the ones that look wrong, and launch full shuttles for credits. A shift lasts five minutes.

  • Grey crate → Bay A
  • White cryo → Bay B
  • Yellow volatile → Bay C
  • Rusty junk → Chute
  • Shaking crate → Scanner

Touch

Left thumb moves, right thumb looks. Tap the round button to act. Swipe up to throw.

Keyboard and mouse

W A S D to move, Shift to walk carefully. Click the view to capture the mouse, or hold and drag to look. E or click to act, Space to throw, P to pause, Esc to free the mouse.
